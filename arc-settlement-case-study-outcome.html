OUTCOME

The document lookup tool launched after I left ARC. The work also had lasting organizational impact, which in legacy transformation is often the harder outcome to achieve.

Product Impact
  • A single document lookup that follows a ticket through exchanges and refunds, launched after my departure
  • Clear rules for editing refunds and exchanges so agents never lose work silently
  • Reduced shadow workflows through shareable state design
Organizational Impact
  • Async testing model adopted org-wide
  • Contributed to internal frameworks formalizing when design research is embedded in product development
  • Service blueprints and journey maps gave teams a shared picture of how a ticket settles
