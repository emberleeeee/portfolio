ARC Settlement / Section 01 of 05

Background

I joined a multi-year initiative at Airlines Reporting Corporation (ARC) to modernize the legacy tools behind transaction reporting and settlement.

What ARC Does

ARC is the financial clearinghouse that sits between airlines and travel agencies. Every refund, exchange, and commission flows through its settlement systems. The accuracy and speed of these tools directly affects airline revenue, agency compliance, and customer trust.

Two legacy tools sat at the center of daily work: an archive of settled transactions, and a reporting tool where agents review and correct transactions during the current weekly cycle. Neither had evolved meaningfully in over two decades.

I owned design for three workstreams:

  • Document lookup.
    A new tool for finding any ticket by its document number and understanding everything that has happened to it.
  • The agency reporting and editing tool.
    Modernizing how agents review, correct and authorize their weekly transactions.
  • Transaction file intake.
    Discovery into how ARC receives, validates and troubleshoots the daily transaction files sent by booking systems.
