Team Builder / Section 03 of 06

Key Constraints & System Reality

This was not a greenfield redesign. The solution had to coexist with significant constraints:

  • Invite-to-Join is a legacy system used across the platform and could not be re-architected for this project
  • Team Builder operates in a draft state, while Invite-to-Join assumes finalized actions
  • Some invitation states (e.g., "Invite Needed") exist only in Team Builder and are not visible in the OG invite tool
  • Clubs structure programs differently — some use subprograms as age groups with multiple teams, others use subprograms as individual teams
  • The same player may exist across multiple programs or subprograms simultaneously

Many "obvious" UX proposals — like a single unified invite manager or universal undo — were intentionally deferred to avoid introducing technical risk or misleading users about what actions were truly reversible.
