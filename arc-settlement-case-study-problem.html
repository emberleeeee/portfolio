ARC Settlement / Section 02 of 05

Problem

Core Challenge

How do you modernize a financial compliance workflow that 200+ airlines and agencies depend on daily, without disrupting the precision and timing the industry requires?

A ticket moves through several stages before money changes hands. It's issued through a booking system, reaches ARC in a daily file, can be corrected by the agency during the current weekly cycle, and becomes settled when that cycle closes. Each stage lived in a different place, and the friction compounded at every level:

Fragmented workflows
Agents had the archive and the editing tool open side by side, cross-referenced document numbers by hand, and often resorted to screenshots or spreadsheets to reconcile mismatched data.
Exchanges broke the trail
An exchange or refund creates new documents. The ticket number a customer quotes might be settled history while the answer they want sits on a newer document in a different tool.
UI debt
Dense data tables, confusing labels, and cumbersome navigation reflected interfaces that hadn't evolved with user needs in over twenty years.
Terminology misalignment
Labels and status indicators meant different things across tools and teams, causing misinterpretation at points where errors had real financial consequences.
Manual work behind the scenes
Operations teams tracked incoming transaction files in spreadsheets, switched between many applications to troubleshoot errors, and often couldn't resolve an issue without waiting on another team.
No culture of validation
User-centered discovery had not been embedded in ARC's product process. Building that foundation was as much a part of the work as the UI itself.
